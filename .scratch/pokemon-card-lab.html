/*
================================================================================
SIMEY POKÉMON CARD RENDERING ARCHITECTURE: EVOLUTION & COMPARISON
================================================================================

1. CODEPEN V1 (PrQKgo) — "Original Holographic Pokémon Cards":
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • Single DOM node (.card) relying exclusively on CSS pseudo-elements:
     - .card:before  => Specular glare highlight (radial gradient tracking pointer)
     - .card:after   => Holographic rainbow striping (repeating-linear-gradient)
   • Blend Mode: color-dodge on :after with background-size multiplier.
   • Limitations: Fixed portrait card layout; art and borders baked into background;
     no support for foil textures, masks, cosmos patterns, or individual rarities.

2. CODEPEN V2 (abYWJdX) & GITHUB REPO (pokemon-cards-css) — "V2 Multi-Pass Holo":
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • Dedicated 3-layer DOM hierarchy:
     - .card__front (holds printed card face image)
     - .card__shine (composite multi-pass shader with base, ::before, and ::after)
     - .card__glare (specular highlight with pointer-responsive falloff)
   • 23 distinct rarity recipes (Beam, Cosmos, Secret Gold, Radiant, VMAX, etc.).
   • Dynamic CDN Assets (poke-holo.b-cdn.net):
     - --foil: Upscaled etched foil textures (geometric, sunpillar, ancient).
     - --mask: Upscaled SVG/alpha clipping masks isolating the foil effect to
       the illustration window while keeping text/stats flat matte.
   • Svelte Spring Physics: Natural inertia on rotation, glare, and background shifts.

3. OUR LANDSCAPE PROJECT CARD ADAPTATION:
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   • Architectural Separation:
     - Holographic passes (.projectShine, .projectGlare) are strictly confined
       to the visual illustration panel (.art).
     - Semantic project metadata (title, tags, description, live links) lives
       outside the shader pipeline (.copy) for 100% accessibility and crisp text.
   • Shader Adaptations:
     - Stripped portrait-only clip paths (--clip, --clip-stage, --clip-trainer).
     - Decoupled pointer tracking: card-level 3D rotation across whole article,
       with localized specular highlight and foil angle inside .art.
     - Preserves full recipe language (mix-blend-mode, gradient formulas,
       color stops, texture blend modes).
     - Respects prefers-reduced-motion by disabling 3D transforms while keeping
       interactive inspection accessible.

================================================================================
UPSTREAM (pokemon-cards-css) VS OUR HTML LAB: FUNCTION-BY-FUNCTION MATRIX
================================================================================

1. POINTER INTERACTION (Math.js & Card.svelte):
   • Upstream `interact(e)`:
     - Calculates pointer coords relative to element bounding rect.
     - Maps `x`, `y` from [0, 100] to center delta `[-50, 50]`.
     - Maps `--background-x` to [37%, 63%], `--background-y` to [33%, 67%].
     - Calculates `--pointer-from-center`, `--pointer-from-top`, `--pointer-from-left`.
   • Our Lab `pointer()` & `pointerProject()`:
     - EXACT SAME mathematical mapping applied to `--pointer-x`, `--pointer-y`,
       `--background-x`, `--background-y`, `--pointer-from-center`, `--card-opacity`.
     - Enhanced for Landscape: Dual-coordinate tracking. Whole-card bounding box
       drives 3D card perspective; inner art panel box drives localized specular glare.

2. CLICK ACTIVATION & POPOVER (Card.svelte `activate`, `popover`, `retreat`):
   • Upstream:
     - `activate(e)` sets `$activeCard = thisCard`.
     - `popover()` zooms card to screen center (scale up to 1.75x or screen fit).
     - First pop triggers 360° horizontal spin flip (`rotateDelta.x: 360deg`).
     - `box-shadow` illuminates card with type-specific energy glow (`--card-glow`).
     - Clicking again or clicking outside triggers `retreat()`.
   • Our Lab:
     - Implemented via `togglePopover()`, `popoverCard()`, `retreatCard()`.
     - Clicking `#pokemon` or `#project` (or pressing Enter/Space) activates modal
       zoom with backdrop blur, 360° spin flip animation, and type-specific glow aura!
     - Escape key or backdrop click dismisses smoothly.

3. SHOWCASE AUTO-WOBBLE (Card.svelte `showcase` onMount loop):
   • Upstream:
     - 20ms interval advancing angle `r += 0.05`.
     - Oscillates rotation: `rotate: { x: sin(r)*25, y: cos(r)*25 }`.
     - Oscillates glare: `glare: { x: 55 + sin(r)*55, y: 55 + cos(r)*55, o: 0.8 }`.
   • Our Lab:
     - Implemented via `startShowcase()` / `stopShowcase()` and "▶ Auto Showcase" button.
     - Runs the identical sinusoidal 3D oscillation loop across cards hands-free!
     - Pauses gracefully on manual hover.

4. DEVICE ORIENTATION / GYROSCOPE (orientation.js & Card.svelte `orientate`):
   • Upstream:
     - Listens to `deviceorientation` event (`beta`, `gamma`).
     - Clamps tilt to [-16, 16] and [-18, 18] degrees.
   • Our Lab:
     - Implemented via `window.addEventListener('deviceorientation')` with the same
       relative angle math. Mobile visitors tilting their device experience physical tilt.

5. CARD CLASSIFIER & ASSET MAPPER (CardProxy.svelte `classify`, `foilMaskImage`):
   • Upstream:
     - Classifies 91 cards across 23 rarities (Shiny Vault, Trainer Gallery, Promos, etc.).
     - Generates CDN URLs: `${cdn}/foils/${set}/${type}/upscaled/${num}_foil_${etch}_${style}_2x.webp`.
   • Our Lab:
     - Implemented in `classify(c)` and `sourceCard(card)` covering all 91 records.
     - Procedural random seed (`--seedx`, `--seedy`, `--cosmosbg`) matched to upstream.

6. LAYER DECOMPOSITION & SEPARATION (Our Lab Innovation):
   • Upstream: Static 3D rotator button without exploded inspection controls.
   • Our Lab:
     - "Separate layers" toggles full 3D Z-axis stack separation:
       Face (0px) -> Illustration (16px) -> Phone (42px) -> Foil (60px) -> Glare (85px).
     - Independent layer toggles (Base, ::before, ::after, Glare, Foil, Mask, Art).
