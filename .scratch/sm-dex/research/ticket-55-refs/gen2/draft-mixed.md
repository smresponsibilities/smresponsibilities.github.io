# Gen II reference addendum

Verified 20 additional visual references beyond `restart/catalogue.json` and ticket 47. Originals are saved unchanged beside the index as `gen2/*`; SHA-1 values in `gen2.json` match the MediaWiki imageinfo hashes and downloaded files.

The strongest hardware references are the Gold and Crystal *Pokémon Adventures* panels, the two Johto handbook artworks, the *Neo Genesis* era TCG illustration, the *Pocket Monsters* panels, and the GSC entry screenshot. These show recurring landmarks: a tall closed body, central circular lens, lower blue lens, top and side leaves, recessed screen, D-pad, paired bar keys, round keys, hinge barrels, and antenna.

`Pokédex Adventures.png` is a mixed multi-device manga panel. It is included as a comparison only because generation assignment is not explicit. `Pokédex scan EToP.png` is a small, continuity-unclear manga crop and should be treated as low-confidence.

The seven English Stadium 2 and three Japanese Stadium Pokédex captures are distinct Gen II-era software views, included to document list/detail/search/order/zoom states. They are software references, not casing evidence. The Japanese and English captures are different language releases and different screens; they are not image resizes.

Excluded from this addendum: `JohtoDex2.png` (HGSS), the GS flyer (repeats known artwork), the Tiger Deluxe angles (already in ticket-55 candidates), `Pokédex Holders GSC.png` (group panel with no readable Pokédex), and the two `Pokédex PM` files where one is a crop/resized repeat of the other.

Sources: Bulbagarden Archives file pages and direct originals linked per record. Visual inspection performed on all 20 downloaded files before inclusion.
