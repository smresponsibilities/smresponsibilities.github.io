# Visual review of the additional references

Reviewed the three 20-image overview boards and compared the new selections with the
earlier catalogue's anime captures. The references are scan states and selected device
views. They do not provide 20 different mechanical angles per generation.

## Generation II

The wider Hoothoot, Skarmory, Miltank and Kingdra frames show the lower screen above the
directional pad and keys, with a separate right leaf and exposed lower lens. Tight crops
such as Swinub are useful for image-to-bezel spacing only. Screen fields vary between
pink, yellow, blue and green. The dark border remains distinct from the red shell.
These observations support the repaired control clearance. The study's portfolio menu
uses ticket 35's readable dark display treatment rather than imitating a Pokémon scan.

## Generation IV

The frames distinguish the red device's dark surround from the pink device's pale
surround. Cranidos and Onix views expose the lower display and side controls; most other
views favor the upper display. The two EP484 frames and two EP606 frames contain different
scan subjects. They are separate frames, not alternate crops counted twice.

The study keeps its chosen color interpretation, with the aperture aligned to its actual
bezel and the controls outside the display. The captures do not justify changing the
moving-part ownership restored in ticket 53.

## Generation V

The gray rails, dark inset frame, green edge key, upper scan window and colorful lower
readout are consistent visible landmarks. Many captures crop out the lower circular
control, so they cannot establish its geometry. The Nonomi frame adds a rear/handheld
context view of a pale variant.

The first draft's Snivy image repeated the original `g5-09` frame at another resolution.
It was rejected during visual review, despite having a different file hash. A proposed
EP662 replacement also repeated the original `g5-06` handheld view and was rejected. Final
selection and checks are recorded in `gen5.json` and `verification.json`.

Gray screen rails in these cropped views are compatible with a colored lower shell.
They do not establish an all-gray colorway. The official source for the selected
red-orange BW correction remains `../ticket-55-ui-review.md`.
