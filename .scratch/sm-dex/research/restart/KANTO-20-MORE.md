# Twenty additional Kanto references

Retrieved and visually inspected on 2026-09-03 for the selection `g1-01`, `g1-05`, and `g1-06`.

## Verified scope

This set contains 20 additional original-series animation frames. Each file page identifies its episode. The archive APIs supply the native image URLs, dimensions, byte counts, and SHA1 values recorded in [kanto-extra-candidates.json](kanto-extra-candidates.json). Nineteen frames come from WikiDex and one comes from Poképédia. These sites archive the original animation; neither site is its original publisher.

All twenty native files were inspected. Their local SHA1 values match the archive metadata, all twenty hashes are unique, and none matches a hash in the existing 90-item catalogue. A visual comparison against `g1-01`, `g1-05`, and `g1-06` found no duplicate counted as another resolution or crop. The EP001 blank-screen upload and EP064 Mr. Mime uploads were excluded because the selected references already cover those states.

Most additions are display states, not independent casing angles. No remake device, later-generation shell, manga, fan drawing, or merchandise is included. This is evidence for a reconstruction, not an approved reconstruction.

## Findings that change the build

### Closed exterior is now visible

[`k1-01`, the EP001 trainer-kit shot](https://www.pokepedia.fr/Fichier:Episode_1_-_Pok%C3%A9dex.png) shows the closed red cover in Oak's hand. The blue lens and top header remain exposed above the lid. The cover has a small yellow triangular mark and lower slots. The user's earlier request to omit the triangle would be a deliberate adaptation. This still does not show the back face, latch operation, or full hinge motion.

### Physical contact and identity content appear together

[`k1-05`, the EP002 identity shot](https://www.wikidex.net/wiki/Archivo:EP002_Pok%C3%A9dex_mostrando_la_informaci%C3%B3n_de_Ash.png) shows Ash's portrait in the main screen while a gloved finger contacts the small yellow right-side button. The entire open device is visible, including distinct blue grid keys, white keys, the left round control, and the D-pad. This supports the portfolio profile concept and separate pressable controls. The still alone does not prove causality, press timing, or the exact function of every button.

### The selected sources disagree about controls

The existing `g1-05` blank-screen frame lacks the lower-left round control and D-pad shown in the RG illustration `g1-01`. The additional EP002, EP010, EP013, EP034, and EP046 frames show these controls. Treat the missing controls in `g1-05` as a source discrepancy, not a reason to make the portfolio controls appear and disappear. See [EP002](https://www.wikidex.net/wiki/Archivo:EP002_Pok%C3%A9dex_mostrando_la_informaci%C3%B3n_de_Ash.png), [EP010](https://www.wikidex.net/wiki/Archivo:EP010_Bulbasaur_en_la_Pok%C3%A9dex.png), and [EP013](https://www.wikidex.net/wiki/Archivo:EP013_Krabby_en_la_Pok%C3%A9dex.png).

The RG illustration also puts speaker lines above its screen, while the anime's white bezel puts them at lower right. One casing cannot match both depictions pixel for pixel. Choose one consistent bezel and control inventory before rendering the open and closed artwork.

### Visible scan feedback already exists in the source

[`k1-14`, the Squirtle scan](https://www.wikidex.net/wiki/Archivo:EP012_Pok%C3%A9dex_analizando_a_Squirtle.png) shows the raised blue lens glowing toward the target. [The Spearow shot](https://www.wikidex.net/wiki/Archivo:EP001_Spearow_en_la_Pokedex.jpg) also shows a bright lens while the species appears on screen. A contained button ripple would be a portfolio interaction addition, not something proved by these stills. Keep the button's feedback separate from the source's lens glow.

### Display backgrounds are content, not power state

The [Lickitung screen](https://www.wikidex.net/wiki/Archivo:EP052_Lickitung_en_la_Pok%C3%A9dex.png) is black behind an active species image. Other entries use pale blue, yellow, or green backgrounds. Do not equate a black pixel region with the device being powered off. The [Mewtwo scan](https://www.wikidex.net/wiki/Archivo:EP063_Mewtwo_en_la_Pok%C3%A9dex.png) uses white horizontal marks on a dark blue screen; the marks cannot be transcribed as readable text from this frame.

## Original publisher playback references

The official Pokémon TV channel publishes [EP001, Pokémon - I Choose You!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z3hMX65Khtg). This is the preferred next source for checking the order of opening, finger contact, display response, and closing. Its chapter marker at 10:31 begins the Pidgey section; that marker is not a verified timestamp for a hinge or button action.

The same channel publishes [The Ghost of Maiden's Peak](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=boY4QaYhBeM) and [The Battle of the Badge](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VQFtkKgOVGk), which can cross-check the ghost scan and unknown-Mewtwo states. Channel season numbering differs from archive episode codes, so keep the episode title with each citation.

These videos are follow-up verification sources, not extra images counted toward the twenty. The full videos and their opening/closing sequences were not inspected in this research pass.

## What remains unresolved

- A continuous, inspected open-to-close sequence. A closed still cannot define hinge travel by itself.
- A source-backed function for every visible key.
- The final RG-versus-anime bezel and control inventory. The selected references disagree.
- Exact screen boundaries, perspective correction, and proved alignment between shell artwork and coded buttons.
- Any interactive implementation claim. This research did not test ripple timing, gaps, keyboard input, or button travel.

Do not call this pack pixel-perfect casing artwork. It provides the source evidence needed to build and compare that artwork.
